Some people believe that governments should spend more money on public transport than on building new roads. Others think the opposite. In my opinion, funding public transport brings clear benefits to cities, although it is not without cost.

Firstly, when the government invests in buses and trains, fewer people need to drive. This means fewer cars on the road and cleaner air. For example, in my city, air pollution fell after a new metro line opened.

However, roads are also important because many areas still depend on cars. If roads are not maintained, traffic becomes worse and businesses suffer.

Overall, I think governments should prioritise public transport, but they should not ignore road maintenance completely.
