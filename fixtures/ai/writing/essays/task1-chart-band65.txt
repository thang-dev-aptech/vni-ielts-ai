The chart shows coffee and tea consumption in millions of kilograms between 2010 and 2020. Overall, coffee consumption rose steadily while tea consumption declined after 2015.

Coffee started at about 40 million kg in 2010 and increased to roughly 70 million by 2020. The growth was gradual with no major drops.

Tea began at a similar level to coffee but fell sharply after 2015, ending at around 30 million kg in 2020. By contrast, coffee continued to climb throughout the period.

In summary, coffee became more popular over the decade, whereas tea lost ground in the second half of the period.
